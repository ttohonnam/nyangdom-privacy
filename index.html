Last updated: November 22, 2026

We do not directly collect or store personally identifiable information on our own servers. However, the App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you or provide game features:

  • Google AdMob: Used to display advertisements (interstitial and rewarded ads). AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as Advertising ID) and usage data for ad delivery and analytics.

2. How Information Is Used

  • To provide leaderboard rankings and gameplay services.
  • To serve and measure advertisements.
  • To verify and restore in-app purchases.

3. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our official channels (@ttohonnam) or the developer email listed on the Google Play Store page.
